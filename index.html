Integrated SOP to prevent revenue leakage and maintain breakfast service standards

Front Office (FO)

Verify Room Only (RO) and Complimentary / House Use (HU) rooms upon check-in. If over-pax covers are spotted at the outlet, DM immediately posts the charge to the guest folio prior to checkout.

SLA: Zero Unbilled Checkout
F&B Service (Greeter)

Hostess must cross-check room numbers against the Night Audit Expected Breakfast List. Guests without breakfast entitlement must be directed to the cashier for settlement.

SLA: 100% Door Verification
Kitchen (Executive Chef)

Utilize the average Turnout Ratio (88%-92%) to forecast daily buffet food preparation. Never prep 100% of system entitlement to prevent excessive food waste.

SLA: Food Waste < 8%
Finance / Night Audit

Conduct joint reconciliation at 10:30 AM comparing POS cashier bills, hostess door sheets, and PMS DRR. Daily reconciliation closes no later than 11:30 AM WITA.

SLA: Closing at 11:30 AM
